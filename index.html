How the credit system works

Every paid plan includes a monthly credit pool equal to the plan price. Credits deplete based on which AI models you use.

⚡

Auto Mode

Cursor picks the most cost-efficient model automatically. Unlimited and doesn't meaningfully drain your credit pool for everyday use.

🧠

Frontier Models

Manually selecting Claude Sonnet, GPT-4o, or Gemini draws from your credit pool. $20 in Pro credits = ~225 Claude requests or ~500 GPT requests.

📊

On-Demand Overages

Once your credits are exhausted, you can keep going. Overages are billed at the same API rates — no markup, billed in arrears.

🔒

Privacy Mode

Enable Privacy Mode to guarantee that your code is never stored by model providers or used for training. Available on all paid plans.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Hobby plan is free forever with no credit card required. It includes limited Agent requests, Tab completions, and 50 slow premium model requests per month. There's also a 7-day Pro trial bundled in. University students can get free Pro access for a year with a .edu email.

What payment methods are accepted?

Self-serve plans accept all major credit and debit cards. For invoice-based billing and wire transfers, contact us to discuss the Enterprise plan.

How does annual billing work?

Annual billing saves 20% across all paid tiers. Pro goes from $20/month to $16/month ($192/year). You're billed once per year upfront.

What happens when I run out of credits?

You can continue using Cursor. On-demand usage lets you keep using models after your included credits are consumed. Overages are billed at standard API rates with no penalty markup, in arrears at the end of the month.

Can I switch plans at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

Do Teams users get the same models as Pro?

Yes. Every Teams seat gets Pro-equivalent AI access — the same frontier models, agent capabilities, and credit pool — plus organizational features like shared rules, centralized billing, SSO, and admin controls.

What is Privacy Mode?

When Privacy Mode is enabled, Cursor guarantees your code data is never stored by model providers or used for training. It can be enabled in settings or enforced org-wide by a team admin. See our Security page for details.

When should I choose Enterprise over Teams?

Choose Enterprise if you need SCIM 2.0 provisioning, audit logs for compliance, pooled usage credits across your organization, invoice/wire billing, or a dedicated support SLA. Contact sales to get a custom quote.
